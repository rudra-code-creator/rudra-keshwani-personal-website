### 2/9 — IntelliGIS
Image: `01-intelligis.png`

IntelliGIS is my geospatial AI product: ask plain-English questions of maps, satellite imagery, and planning data without traditional GIS complexity.

I founded it to make AI-powered geospatial analysis more accessible — product strategy, architecture, customer discovery, and early go-to-market all in one loop. The MVP lets you prompt against a live map context (think master-plan style questions over a city) instead of fighting layers and toolbars.

Live: https://intelligis-mvp.netlify.app
Code: https://github.com/rudra-code-creator/intelliGIS-MVP

### 3/9 — World Metro Visualiser
Image: `02-world-metro-visualiser.png`

World Metro Visualiser is for exploring and comparing metro systems worldwide — lines, stations, and network layouts across major cities in one interactive map.

It’s the kind of project I love: dense spatial data, clear visual hierarchy, and a UI that makes “how does Beijing’s network compare?” feel obvious at a glance.

Live: https://world-metro-visualiser.netlify.app/
Code: https://github.com/rudra-code-creator/world-metro-visualiser

### 4/9 — Leap Ahead
Image: `03-leap-ahead.png`

Leap Ahead came out of the UQIES × January Capital × Lovable Hackathon 2026.

It’s AI career guidance for students and early-career builders — personalized pathways, skill gaps, weekly focus, and opportunities in a student-hub style dashboard. Built fast under hackathon constraints, but aimed at a real problem: turning “I don’t know what to do next” into a concrete plan.

Live: https://leap-uqies-hackathon-sandy.vercel.app/
Code: https://github.com/rudra-code-creator/leap-UQIES-hackathon

### 5/9 — Airline Data Explorer
Image: `04-airline-data-explorer.png`

Airline Data Explorer is interactive aviation analytics — airline networks, routes, airports, and global connectivity patterns.

It’s a local Python app (clone → venv → run). First launch may take longer while data caches build. Great if you want to dig into fleet / route structure without a black-box dashboard.

Code + setup: https://github.com/rudra-code-creator/airline-data-explorer

### 6/9 — FlightRadar67
Image: `05-flightradar67.png`

FlightRadar67 is a real-time aviation radar on a full CesiumJS globe.

Live ADS-B traffic loads in viewport chunks, aircraft are colour-coded by altitude, and selecting a flight reveals its flown trail with route context — more radar screen than dashboard. Filters, airport markers, callsigns, and geolocation make it feel like an ops console, not a toy map.

Live: https://flightradar67.netlify.app
Code: https://github.com/rudra-code-creator/flightradar67

### 7/9 — Arya Clark Luxury Real Estate
Image: `06-arya-clark.png`

Client-facing marketing site for luxury real estate agent Arya Clark.

Dark theme, gold accents, full-bleed estate photography, and a clear path to book an appraisal — plus featured homes, property browse, map, and services. Built to feel premium without getting in the way of the inventory and the CTA.

Live: https://arya-clark-website.vercel.app

### 8/9 — Personal website
Image: `07-personal-website.png`

My own site is the home base for everything else: profile + TIBER framework, experience, skills, resume (view/download), featured projects carousel, LinkedIn posts, travel map, life checklist, blog, and a pop quiz — with light/dark theme, sidebar nav, and a command palette.

Stack: Next.js 15, TypeScript, Tailwind. Deployed via GitHub → Vercel.

Live: https://rudra-keshwani-personal-website.vercel.app/
Code: https://github.com/rudra-code-creator/rudra-keshwani-personal-website
